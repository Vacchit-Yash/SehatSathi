Public healthcare navigation and visit-preparation assistant

How can we help?

This demo helps you prepare for a public-health facility visit. It does not diagnose illness, prescribe medicines, or confirm live facility availability. Do not enter names, Aadhaar numbers, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers.

Emergency? If someone may be in immediate danger, has severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, unconsciousness, uncontrolled bleeding, or another life-threatening condition, do not use this form. Call India’s emergency number 112 or go to the nearest emergency department now.

Important limitations

  • This demo does not search a live directory or identify the nearest facility.
  • Facility services, timings, eligibility and required documents vary by location and can change.
  • Document suggestions below are preparation ideas, not universal mandatory requirements.
  • Confirm details with the relevant government facility, official state health portal, or community health worker.
